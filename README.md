# 🔥 Non-Negotiables-Habit-Tracker

A minimalist habit tracker built for people who are tired of starting over.

Most people don't fail because they lack motivation.

They fail because motivation disappears.

This tracker was built around a simple idea:

> **The life you want is hidden inside the things you do every day.**

No complicated systems.
No endless productivity hacks.
No gamification overload.

Just your daily non-negotiables.

---

## Why This Exists

We all have goals.

* Get healthier
* Read more
* Exercise consistently
* Study without procrastinating
* Become the person we keep promising ourselves we'll become

The problem is that goals are occasional.

Habits are daily.

This tracker helps you focus on the small actions that quietly shape your future.

## Philosophy

A habit done imperfectly is still better than a habit skipped entirely.

You don't need to be perfect.

You only need to show up today.

One checkbox.

One habit.

One day at a time.
